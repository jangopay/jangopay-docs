# Product overview

JangoPay’s website presents six product areas. Availability and configuration depend on the current product and your account.

| Product | Purpose | Typical workflow |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Payment links | Create and share a hosted payment page | Collect payment without a custom checkout |
| API & Webhooks | Connect payment records and status notifications | Synchronize payment and business orders |
| Collections | Track collection records and payment status | Review confirmed, pending and exceptional payments |
| Treasury & transfers | Review asset balances and transfer records | Move available assets to an external wallet |
| Exchange | Convert supported assets | Convert eligible volatile assets into stablecoins |
| Customer insights | Understand visits, payments and conversion | Review customer and payment trends |

## One workspace, distinct responsibilities

A payment record, asset balance, transfer and customer metric serve different purposes. Keep their identifiers and reconciliation rules separate in your own systems.

## What this guide does not promise

This overview is not a custody statement, exchange-rate guarantee, settlement-time commitment or exhaustive asset list. Consult current product terms, account settings and support before making operational assumptions.
