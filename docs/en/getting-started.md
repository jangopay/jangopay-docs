# Getting started

## 1. Choose your collection method

Start with a **payment link** when you need a simple page to share with customers. Choose **API & Webhooks** when payment records and notifications need to connect to your website, application or backend.

## 2. Create a payment

Sign in through [the official website](https://www.jangopay.org/). Create a collection record using the options available in your account. Check the amount, order description and supported assets/networks before sharing the payment page.

## 3. Guide your customer

1. Review the amount and select a supported asset/network combination.
2. Scan the QR code or copy the displayed address.
3. Send the payment using the same network and the payment page’s instructions.
4. Wait for confirmation and review the payment result.

## 4. Confirm and reconcile

Use verified payment status in the merchant workspace or your documented integration. A customer screenshot or transaction submission alone is not a confirmed business payment. Match the payment record to your own order before delivery.

## 5. Plan exceptions

Define how your team handles expiration, short or excess payments, unsupported networks and delayed confirmation. Use the official support process; do not assume a universal refund or recovery policy.
